01 — THE FOUNDATION

Understanding data.
Understanding the world.

Statistics gives structure to information. Probability helps us reason about what may happen. Together, they create a foundation for informed decisions.

01 / COURSE OVERVIEW
⌁

A framework for evidence

This portfolio organizes key concepts in probability and statistics into a guided collection of modules and lessons. It connects mathematical ideas with practical interpretation and data-driven thinking.

FROM RAW DATA TO REASONED CONCLUSIONS
◎

Learning objectives

Organize and summarize data, calculate statistical measures, apply probability rules, interpret distributions, and communicate conclusions clearly.

⌘

Why it matters in computer science

Statistical thinking supports data science, machine learning, software testing, performance analysis, research, and evidence-based system decisions.

◌

From description to inference

Descriptive statistics summarize what has been observed. Inferential statistics use sample evidence to estimate or test claims about a wider population.
